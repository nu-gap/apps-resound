The Tools Menu

The Tools Menu allows you to:



Bring up the Console to control sound recording and playback. For more information on the Console, press here: 

Bring up the Sound Inspector to examine and change various aspects of sounds.  There are four inspectors:

The Attributes Inspector deals with sound attributes.

The Selection Inspector deals with the position and
size of selected and viewed sound.

The Zoom Inspector deals with the Zoom (scale) of sound.

The Marks Inspector deals with the sound's ruler,
zero line, amplitude grid, and play mark.

The Text Inspector deals with the sound's accompanying textual information.
